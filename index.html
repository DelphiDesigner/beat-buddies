Beat Buddies

Make beats. Mix them up.

Beat Buddies is a free beat-making app for kids. Drag sounds onto cartoon characters to build a loop, tap out a pattern on a step grid, or play live pads over a beat. Save the beats you make and arrange them across tracks into a full mix.

The app is free, with no ads, no account and no tracking. It was made with love for Ryan.
