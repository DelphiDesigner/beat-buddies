Privacy Policy

Beat Buddies

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Short version: Beat Buddies does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

What the app stores

The app stores the following on your device only: the beats and mixes you make, your settings (such as sound level and haptics), and nothing else. This data is not sent to the developer or to anyone else.

What the app does not do

The app has no account, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party code that collects data. It does not connect to any server owned by the developer. It does not use the microphone.

Sharing

If you choose to export a mix, the app creates an audio file and opens the standard iOS share sheet. You choose where it goes. A grown-up is asked first.

Purchases

Optional tips ("Buy me a coffee") are processed by Apple through In-App Purchase. The developer does not receive your payment details. A grown-up is asked first, and a tip does not unlock anything.

Children

The app is made for children. Because no personal data is collected or sent anywhere, no personal data of children is collected by the developer.

Deleting data

Deleting a beat or mix removes it from the app. Deleting the app removes all its data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
