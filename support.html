Common questions

Where are my saved beats and mixes?
They stay on your device, in the Home tab.

What is the number puzzle before the grown-ups area?
It is a parental gate. A grown-up solves a small task that is easy for an adult and hard for a young child, so settings, tips and sharing stay in grown-up hands.

I changed phones. Where are my beats?
Beats and mixes are stored only on the device. They move to a new phone with an iCloud or computer backup of the old phone.

I left a tip but nothing changed.
Tips do not unlock anything — they only say thank you. "Ask to Buy" tips wait for approval by the family organizer.
